# IP Hygiene Copyright And Database Checks

Load this for copied or adapted text, code, examples, interfaces, schemas,
figures, tables, fixtures, or datasets. Use [authority-index.md](authority-index.md)
for the primary sources. Apply national law when a directive leaves an option
or implementation question.

## Criteria

- **`IP-SRC-1 Source and provenance`:** identify the source, author/holder where
  evidenced, acquisition path, version/date, and whether the reviewed item is
  original, generated, adapted, or copied. An unavailable source is an evidence
  gap, not proof that copying occurred.
- **`IP-SRC-2 Permission`:** Citation records provenance; it is not permission.
  A link, attribution, bibliography entry, or copyright notice does not itself
  authorize reproduction, adaptation, or redistribution.
- **`IP-COPY-1 Expression`:** separate facts, methods, systems, and ideas from
  copied expression. Assess qualitative as well as quantitative taking; do not
  use a word-count safe harbor. Code, prose, examples, figures, and creative
  presentation can each carry expression.
- **`IP-COPY-2 Quotation`:** distinguish lawful quotation from copied expression.
  In the EU framework, verify lawful prior availability, purpose, fair practice,
  the amount required, source/author indication where possible, an identifiable
  quoted work, and the applicable national exception. Citation alone does not
  satisfy these conditions.
- **`IP-COPY-3 Paraphrase`:** an original paraphrase communicates facts, ideas,
  or conclusions in independently created wording and structure. Superficial
  synonym replacement, retained sequencing, or copied expressive choices can
  remain copied expression. Cite influence for provenance even when the new
  expression does not require a licence.
- **`IP-COPY-4 Software and interfaces`:** functional ideas and interfaces,
  program functionality, languages, and data formats are not protected as
  program expression merely because they enable behavior. Copied source/object
  code, expressive documentation, or original expression embedded in a format
  remains a separate question. Interoperability exceptions are fact- and
  jurisdiction-specific.
- **`IP-DB-1 Database copyright`:** database copyright concerns protected selection or arrangement
  that is the author's own intellectual creation; it
  does not arise from labor or investment alone. Contents may have separate
  rights.
- **`IP-DB-2 Sui generis right`:** the sui generis database right is separate
  from database copyright. Establish a qualifying database and substantial
  investment in obtaining, verifying, or presenting contents, then assess
  extraction/re-utilization of a substantial part or repeated systematic use of
  insubstantial parts. Do not infer it from a table's existence.

## Act-Specific Checks

Record whether the proposed act is link, quote, copy, modify, extract,
re-utilize, or redistribute; what exact expression/data moves; the output form;
and its audience. A canonical link generally avoids copying the linked content,
but linking does not cure copied material already bundled locally and may raise
separate questions outside this skill.

For code/config/examples, inspect the actual file-level licence and notices
under [licence-assets.md](licence-assets.md). For schemas, separately assess
functional interface elements, expressive annotations/examples, database
selection/arrangement, and the operative licence. For redrawn figures, ask what
protectable expressive choices were retained; redrawing is not automatically
independent creation.

## Repository Controls

- Prefer original explanations and independently built fixtures over source
  transcription.
- Quote only when a verified licence or applicable exception covers the act and
  amount; record that basis.
- Preserve provenance even when only unprotected facts or ideas are used.
- When source, ownership, exception, or permission is load-bearing and unclear,
  stop the affected decision. Do not label the material infringing or cleared.
