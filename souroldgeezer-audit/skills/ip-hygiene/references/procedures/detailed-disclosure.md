# Detailed Disclosure

Load for an explicitly requested detailed IP report, evaluation, or formal
consumer. Ordinary reports use the compact close in audit-craft section 5.

Keep the formal footer: extensions loaded; tool/MCP availability; reference
paths and authority classes; evidence limits; independence
(`independent | self-review | unknown`); and assurance level
(`prospective bounded decision | limited triage | reasonable-hygiene in-depth`).
For triage, evidence limits identify touched paths, drive-by neighbors examined,
and untouched files not swept. Gate, decision, verdict, and counsel outcomes
retain the owning lane's meanings and precedence.

This is a hygiene assessment, not legal advice, clearance, certification, or an
FTO opinion. A detailed format does not expand the evidence or legal assurance.
