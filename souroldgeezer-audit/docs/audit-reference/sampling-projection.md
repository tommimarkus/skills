# Sampling and projection (shared procedure)

When full enumeration exceeds a feasible budget (Deep/in-depth only).

## Risk-targeted sampling
Full-enumerate all `high`-tier subject surfaces. Sample `medium`/`low` tiers.
Disclose: total population, sample size, and selection basis
(e.g. "risk-targeted: all 12 high-tier, 20% of 140 low-tier").

## Projection
Project local findings to the unexamined population as INFERENCE, not confirmed
fact: "3 of 10 sampled tests in module X assert nothing → likely systemic;
~N of the unexamined ~M plausibly affected." Mark every projection as inference
and never convert it into a confirmed implementation work item.

## Disclosure
The output's assurance-level line (audit-craft.md §4) plus the footer evidence
limits (§5) must state that sampling was used and what was not examined.
Replaces any silent "skipped — suite too large".
